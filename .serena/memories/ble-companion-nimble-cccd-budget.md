NimBLE CCCD budget is already tight in sdf_ble_companion: firmware/sdkconfig sets CONFIG_BT_NIMBLE_MAX_CCCDS=8 and CONFIG_BT_NIMBLE_MAX_BONDS=3, while sdf_ble_companion.c registers 4 NOTIFY-capable characteristics (auth/config/enroll/ota). Worst case persisted-CCCD demand is 4*3=12, already above the 8-slot budget. Evidence: firmware/sdkconfig lines ~1126-1152 and the `s_characteristics[]` array in firmware/components/sdf_ble_companion/src/sdf_ble_companion.c (all four have BLE_GATT_CHR_F_NOTIFY).
Implication for future work: do NOT add a new NOTIFY-capable GATT characteristic to sdf_ble_companion without either raising CONFIG_BT_NIMBLE_MAX_CCCDS or reusing an existing characteristic via an opcode/framing scheme (as done for the BLE OTA chunked-transfer change, see openspec/changes/replace-wifi-ota-with-ble-transfer/design.md). This was the deciding factor for choosing "reuse existing OTA characteristic with opcode prefix" over "add a dedicated OTA data characteristic".
